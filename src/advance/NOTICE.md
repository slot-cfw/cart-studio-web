# Notices for `src/advance`

This module is a modified version, ported to Rust, of the slot. advance label converter by
FrankieT19: `tools/convert_labels.py` and `tools/simple_cartridge.py` in
[FrankieT19/slot-advance](https://github.com/FrankieT19/slot-advance), at the commit in
`slot-advance.ref`. Those files are GPL-3.0-or-later, and this port is distributed under the same
licence as the rest of Cart Studio. The converter's shell palette, header exceptions, overlay model
and simple cartridge faces were themselves adapted by FrankieT19 from slot's `cart.rs`, `shell.rs`,
`bitmaps.rs` and `cart_shell.rs`, by Brandon Kowalski and contributors.

`pillow.rs` ports the LANCZOS resampling (`Resample.c`), RGBA premultiplication (`Convert.c`) and
alpha compositing (`AlphaComposite.c`) of Pillow 11.3.0, together with the logic of `Image.resize`
and `ImageOps.fit`. Pillow is licensed under the MIT-CMU License; its copyright and permission
notice is in `LICENSE-Pillow.txt`.

`cp936.rs` is generated by `tools/cp936_ranges.py` from Python's `cp936` codec.
