# slot. advance assets

Copied unchanged from `assets/slot/` in [FrankieT19/slot-advance](https://github.com/FrankieT19/slot-advance) at the commit in `slot-advance.ref`.

- The cartridge PNGs are GPL-3.0-or-later. Realistic cartridge artwork by Markus (MarkusMaribu).
  Simple cartridge moulds from slot by Brandon Kowalski and contributors. Tinting and layering for
  slot. advance by FrankieT19.
- `ds-font.bin` is the 6x12 bitmap font from the official EZ-FLASH OMEGA kernel source
  (`kernels/omega/source/asc126_new.h` in slot. advance), as reused by FrankieT19's DS Style.
